# Hubster Motivations for Ad Creative

**Source:** Jayne Travers-Drapes (Co-founder, Box Divvy)
**Date Received:** March 17, 2026
**Purpose:** Ad creative development reference

---

## Primary Motivations (Why People Become Hubsters)

### 1. Flexibility
- Ability to work from home
- Especially valuable for parents with young children
- Fits around other work or personal commitments
- Provides financial top-up without rigid hours

### 2. Values Alignment
- Box Divvy's mission aligns with personal/family values
- Dislike for how big supermarkets ("Colesworth") treat farmers
- Desire for honesty in food sourcing for consumers
- Anti-corporate, pro-community sentiment

### 3. Supplement Income
- "Top-up" income (not full-time salary replacement)
- Covers groceries and a little extra
- Pays for school activities, even a holiday
- Weekly earnings potential

### 4. Community Connection
- Builds local relationships and social ties
- Being a "local legend" in your town
- Connecting neighbours with fresh food

### 5. Health & Nutrition Passion
- Many Hubsters interested in food, health, nutrition
- Running a Hub complements existing health-focused work
- Access to farm-fresh produce for family

### 6. Family Involvement & Education
- Side gig that involves family members
- Educates family on where food comes from
- Strengthens connection with local community
