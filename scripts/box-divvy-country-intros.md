# Box Divvy: 3x Country-Specific AI Intros

## Purpose
Non-metro Hubster recruitment ads for rural expansion. These intros target potential Hubsters in country Victoria postcodes (3429-3453) along the route from Sunbury to Elphinstone.

---

## Intro 1: "Country Community" Focus
**Tone:** Warm, community-focused, aspirational
**Length:** 8-10 seconds
**Best for:** Community-minded prospects

**Script:**
> "Living in country Victoria, you know the value of real community. What if you could bring fresh, affordable food to your neighbours AND earn income from home? Let me show you how Box Divvy is changing rural food access..."

**Key hook:** Community benefit + income from home

---

## Intro 2: "Rural Hustle" Focus
**Tone:** Practical, opportunity-focused, direct
**Length:** 8-10 seconds
**Best for:** Entrepreneurial prospects

**Script:**
> "Country living shouldn't mean paying city prices for fresh food. Box Divvy lets you run a food hub from your place, serving your local area while building a side income. Here's how it works..."

**Key hook:** Savings + side income

---

## Intro 3: "Local Legend" Focus
**Tone:** Empowering, local pride, action-oriented
**Length:** 8-10 seconds
**Best for:** Social connectors, local leaders

**Script:**
> "Want to be the person who brought better food access to your town? Box Divvy Hubsters are local legends - connecting their communities with farm-fresh produce while earning weekly. Let me explain..."

**Key hook:** Status/recognition + weekly earnings

---

## Usage Notes

### Video Production
- Record each intro with the same presenter/style as existing Box Divvy ads
- Match the visual style: natural lighting, casual/friendly delivery
- Keep energy high but authentic to country/rural tone

### Meta Ads Targeting
- Create separate ad sets for each intro variant
- Target by postcode: 3429, 3427, 3430, 3431, 3434, 3437, 3438, 3440, 3441, 3442, 3444, 3446, 3447, 3448, 3450, 3451, 3453
- A/B test to find best performer by region

### Performance Tracking
- Track CTR and CPL by intro variant
- Track by postcode to identify high-converting areas
- Feed learnings back into future creative development
